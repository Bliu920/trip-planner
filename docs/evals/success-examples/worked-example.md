# Reviewing an I02 saved outcome

This is a review worksheet for [I02](initial.md#i02-complete-single-city-request), not an executed success, travel advice or a serialized fixture. The earlier version used generic meals and period-only schedules. It no longer satisfied the implemented first-plan standard and has been replaced. Actual reference outcomes must come from captured runs, with their evidence, date and source hashes retained.

## Input and observable result

Two adults request Rome for November 4 to 6, 2026, with history and food interests, moderate pace and EUR 800 total excluding flights. The saved Travel brief preserves those sourced requirements and questions. Budget owns the normalized limit and exclusions. The Plan owns the selected schedule and both nights. Original messages remain the source; no raw-input copy or second schedule is required.

A passing candidate has:

- Three practical Rome days, suitable catalog anchors and supporting activities, specific suitable meal venues, rest and realistic movement. No precise arrival or flight is invented.
- Suggested local clock starts plus evidence-based duration or explicit end for the daily timeline. Explicit flexible windows remain flexible. The overview can show Morning/Afternoon/Evening without deleting the clocks.
- Real researched restaurants when catalog meals do not fit. Names, addresses, menu/service evidence and limitations remain available. A daily food allowance does not replace selected meals.
- Both nights assigned to a proposed researched hotel when possible. A provisional area is acceptable when a property cannot be established; it is not a room booking or a verified route endpoint.
- Actual effective itinerary journey findings addressed. Missing map evidence, service timetables, hours and availability remain honest uncertainties. A saved draft can be unverified; it cannot be described as fully checked solely because it saved.
- A priced Budget covering ordinary included costs from numeric evidence, with applicable units, quantities, taxes and exclusions. A genuine research outage may yield an explicitly incomplete draft; that is a resilience result rather than normal complete-budget success.
- A verified final save of the Plan, Budget and dependent files. Research notes and Planning decisions are created only when useful, with independent evidence/choice ownership.
